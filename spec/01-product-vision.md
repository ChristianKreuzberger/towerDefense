# 01 Product Vision

## Goal

Build an offline local multiplayer browser tower-defense game where 1 to 8 named players each place one fixed tower on a procedurally generated map and score points by defending against creature waves.

## Core player fantasy

- Strategic defense planning with teammates
- Fast decisions under pressure
- Meaningful tower build variety
- Replayable maps and wave patterns

## Primary audience

- Players who enjoy strategy and co-op gameplay
- Sessions of 15 to 40 minutes
- Desktop browser first, mobile browser support as a stretch goal
- Couch or party-style local multiplayer on one machine

## Product pillars

1. Cooperative strategy over twitch mechanics
2. Clear game readability and deterministic outcomes
3. Offline-first architecture with online-ready boundaries
4. Content extensibility through data-driven maps and waves

## Game modes at MVP

- Offline local match with 1 to 8 human players
- AI player menu entry visible but disabled (placeholder for future)

## Non-goals at MVP

- Online matchmaking and network play
- PvP ranked ladder
- Hero units
- Complex account economy

## Success metrics

- Day-1 completion rate for first match > 70%
- Match setup completion from menu > 95%
- Average session length 20 minutes or more
- Crash-free sessions > 99%
- Players understand win condition (first to 1000 points) without tutorial help
